# EmptyState

What a page shows before it has data: one small drawing, one sentence, the next action.

**Markup.** `.rt-empty` with `.rt-empty__art` (an SVG built from the mark's bars and dot), `h3`, `p`, and 1–2 buttons.

- The title states the situation plainly ("No shifts this week yet"); the sentence offers the fastest way forward.
- Art is drawn from the mark's vocabulary in tokens (`line-strong`, `brand-tint`, `beat`). No stock illustrations or characters.
- Filtered-to-nothing is not an empty state: say "No results for …" with a Clear filters action.
